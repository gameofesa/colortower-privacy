Privacy Policy

Color Tower · Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information Color Tower ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used. Developer: Enes Kömü. Contact: komuenes969@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, card collection, card and board styles, settings such as sound, music, vibration and language) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data. Friend challenge codes are created on your device and shared only if you choose to share them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and full-screen ads between levels). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; the advertising ID on Android), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, app usage (for example app launches), and crash and performance data of the ad SDK. This data is not linked to your identity by us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

On iOS the game asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) lets you choose how your data is used for ads; you can change your choice any time in the game under Settings › Privacy Options.

Purchases

The game offers optional one-time in-app purchases (coin packs and a 30-day No Ads pack; no subscriptions). Payments are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see or store your payment details. Your purchases are saved only on your device.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The game does not require an account.

Children

Color Tower is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and your choices

Deleting the game removes all game data from your device. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking). We do not sell your personal information.

Changes

If we update this policy we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
